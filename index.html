PeakCut

AI clipping for live streams and videos

PeakCut watches a stream or a video, finds the moments worth keeping, and cuts them into short vertical clips with captions, titles and hooks. It can post the finished clips to a YouTube channel, Instagram account or TikTok account that you connect yourself.

Speech is transcribed on the machine that runs PeakCut. A language model, paid for directly by whoever runs it, picks the moments and writes the wording. PeakCut is free and open source.
